How it works

Waiter

Picks the table and dishes with buttons and taps “Send”.

Kitchen and bar

Each station gets only its items, taps “Accept” and “Ready”; the waiter is notified.

Cashier

Sees which table asked for the bill and closes it: cash, card or transfer.

Owner

Every evening gets the day report in Telegram and a row in Google Sheets.

Questions

Do I need to buy a terminal or tablet?

No. Staff use the phones they already have, with Telegram installed. The kitchen and bar can use any phone or tablet.

Is this a fiscal cash register?

No. It handles orders, the kitchen, bills and day reports. Fiscal receipts stay with your existing cash register or card terminal.

How long does the launch take?

Usually 48 hours: I load your menu, set up stations and staff, and show the team how to use it.

Can I change the menu myself?

Yes. The menu lives in a Google Sheet: edit names and prices there and the bot picks them up.

What languages are supported?

English, Russian and Georgian. Each employee chooses their own language.
